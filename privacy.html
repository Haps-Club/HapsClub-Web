Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Overview

What this covers.

Haps Club ("Haps," "we," "us") publishes a weekly guide to events in Los Angeles. This policy covers the personal information we collect through haps.club, our newsletter signup, and our social and ad campaigns.

Information we collect

Name and email, nothing more.

When you subscribe to our newsletter or fill out a signup form — including through Facebook or Instagram ads — we collect your name and email address. We don't collect payment information, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

How we use it

To send you the newsletter. That's it.

We use your information to send you our newsletter and occasional updates about Haps Club events. We do not sell or rent your information to third parties.

Third-party services

Kit and Meta process some of this for us.

We use Kit (ConvertKit) to send our newsletter, and Meta (Facebook/Instagram) to run ads and process signups through Instant Forms. These providers process your data under their own privacy policies.

Your choices

Unsubscribe anytime, or ask us to delete it.

Every newsletter includes an unsubscribe link. You can also email us at hello@haps.club to request that we delete your information.

Cookies

Basic analytics only.

Our website may use basic analytics cookies to understand traffic. No personal information is sold based on this data.

Contact

Questions about this policy.

Email us at hello@haps.club.
