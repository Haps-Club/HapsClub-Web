Dinner, jazz, and all of Halloween

Dinner, jazz, a month of Halloween, and one last free Saturday on the Santa Monica Pier.

This week has a dinner, a night of jazz, a costume party for every kind of person, and one last free Saturday of Wellness & Waves on the Santa Monica Pier.

This week at a glance

Haps pick · South LA · 10% off this week

Haps Underground Supper Club: Masala Meets Mexico

A cozy vegetarian dinner where Indian and Mexican street food meet at one candlelit table.

 

A playful multi-course dinner with cardamom horchata, hibiscus-tamarind pani puri, grilled paneer tostadas, and a persimmon and roasted pepita salad. It is hands-on, communal, and meant for meeting new people. Fully non-alcoholic with festive drinks, so no BYOB. The address goes to confirmed guests.

Haps event · Jazz Age Party · 10% off

Haps Club Jazz Age Party

An underground jazz club in 1926 Los Angeles, with a live trio.

 

A historic home turns 100, and we’re celebrating Gatsby style: a live jazz trio, candlelight, an open bar of Prohibition-era cocktails, and lite bites all night. Come in your Jazz Age finest. No casual attire, and please arrive on time.

Outdoors · Santa Monica · Free

Wellness & Waves on the Santa Monica Pier

A free group run and yoga by the ocean, open to every level.

 

The run leaves at 8 from the top of the pier at Colorado and Ocean, then yoga and yoga-pilates fusion run from 9 to 10 at the west end. Bring a mat and a towel. This is the final session of the season. Event page.

Active · Santa Monica · $30 a player

The Santa Monica Open: Pickleball

Start the doubles partner search now, this one is three weeks out.

 

A community tournament with women’s and men’s doubles on Saturday and open and mixed doubles on Sunday, welcome to all ages and levels. Registration is through the Swish app. Register here.

Culture · Santa Monica · $20 RSVP

Kiwis in LA Art Show & Wine Tasting

A taste of Aotearoa New Zealand culture on the Westside.

 

An art show and wine tasting from Kiwis in LA, with 100% of proceeds going to the group. Entry at the door is free if space allows. Clark will be there, so come say hi. RSVP here.

The Halloween edit

Pumpkins, haunted houses, and costume parties

Three ways to spend the spookiest month of the year, in date order.

 

Halloween · La Cañada Flintridge · Through Nov 1

Carved at Descanso Gardens

Hundreds of hand-carved pumpkins and a glowing trail through the garden after dark.

 

An illuminated railroad, whimsical wood spirits, and kid-friendly activities along the trail. Tickets are timed, so book ahead. Get tickets.

Halloween · Hollywood · Costume contest

The Haunted Mansion: Halloween Costume Party

DJs, live performers, and a real haunted house at a secret Hollywood location.

 

Multiple rooms, several bars, and a prize for the best costume of the night. The DJ lineup is still to be announced. Paid parking, and refunds are available up to 7 days before. Tickets on Eventbrite.

Halloween · Jefferson Park · Free to attend

Garden Halloween Party with Asado

Latin American darkwave, electro, and post-punk in a backyard garden.

 

The Gauchos Asado Argentino will be selling bites and drinks. Street parking only, so please be kind to the neighbors. Come dressed to impress, because the best costume wins free entry to a Domingo de Asado at the garden. See the post.

Road trip · Oak Glen · 1–2 hours

Apple picking in Oak Glen

Orchards, cider, and fall air, an hour or two from most of LA.

 

October is prime U-pick season, and apples thin out late, so go early. Los Rios Rancho opened its corn maze on September 20. Hours vary by orchard, so check before you drive, and weekdays are quieter. Where to pick.
