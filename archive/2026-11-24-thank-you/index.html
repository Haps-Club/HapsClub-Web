Thank you

25+ events, 1,500+ of you, and we’re just getting started. Plus a December dinner and your Thanksgiving week.

Hi there, it’s Thanksgiving week, so I want to start with the people who made this year happen.

A lot of us moved here for work, watched our friends get busy, or know the feeling of walking into a room alone. Haps started as a simple bet that Los Angeles had plenty of people who wanted to meet each other and just needed a reason to show up. Every event we host lives on our website, and we post regularly on Instagram.

My favorite moment never changes: somewhere around the second drink, a group of people who just met starts laughing like they’ve known each other for years. Every one of you who came out made that happen, and we’re just getting started. If you know people who’d want this in their inbox, send them here to subscribe.
