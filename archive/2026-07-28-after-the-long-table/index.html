After the Long Table

A great dinner behind us, a full week ahead, and two September dates to lock in.

Hi, thank you to everyone who came to our first Long Table dinner on Saturday. One communal table, candlelight, and a room that did not want to leave. The next one is already in the works, and the WhatsApp group hears about it first.

This week we are staying close to the water: a live podcast in Venice, a movement night in the Marina, new mariscos on Abbot Kinney, movies on the sand, and two September dates worth putting on the calendar now.

The lead pick · This Thursday

The Nature of Nurture, live at The KINN

A live podcast taping, a happy hour, and a very good crowd.

Thu Jul 30 · 6 PM happy hour, 7:15 PM taping · The KINN, 150 Horizon Ave, Venice · ticketed

Our friend Leslie Carr hosts a smart podcast on society and culture through a psychological lens, and she is filming a live episode at The KINN in Venice this Thursday, in conversation with Orbitz founder Jeff Katz. Michael will be there, so it is officially a Haps night. Tickets come with a happy hour, and the code Nurture gets you 10% off. Grab a spot.

This week · Thursday

Breaking Ground at Primal Moves

Figure drawing and embodiment, no experience needed.

Thu Jul 30 · 7–10 PM · Primal Moves, 1038 Princeton Dr, Marina del Rey · ticketed

Also shared in the group: chao's figure-drawing and movement night, where a dancer moves through the room in three rounds, mountain, dune, quake, from stillness to sudden motion. All levels welcome, no experience necessary. A different kind of Thursday. Reserve on Luma.

AWOL · Santa Monica

Cinema by the Sea at the Regent

Road-trip movies with the ocean as the back wall.

Sat Aug 1 · Regent Santa Monica Beach · ticketed

The Regent's oceanfront film series marks the Route 66 centennial with road-trip classics, Route 66-inspired cocktails, and unlimited popcorn. This Saturday it is Eat Pray Love, with Little Miss Sunshine and Almost Famous coming later in August. Reserve a spot.

AWOL · Always West of Lincoln · Venice

San Damián on Abbot Kinney

Enrique Olvera's mariscos, feet from the beach.

Open now · 1025 Abbot Kinney Blvd, Venice (former Atla space) · Resy

The chef behind Pujol and Damián turned the long-empty Atla space into a modern marisquería, ceviches, fish tacos, and fresh tortillas steps from the boardwalk. See the menu and book a table.

AWOL · Venice

Long Run Saturday with Venice Run Club

Six or ten miles, then the whole parking lot hangs out.

Sat 8:30 AM (also Wed 6:30 PM) · 2150 Dell Ave, Venice · free, no sign-up

The Westside's big friendly run club keeps it simple: a 4.5-miler on Wednesday evenings and a Saturday long run with 6 and 10+ mile options, all paces welcome. Show up at the lot on Dell and fall in. Full schedule.

Coming up · Sep 26–27

Ocean Way Festival

The pier gets a proper festival, finally.

Sat–Sun Sep 26–27 · Santa Monica Beach · two-day, all ages · ticketed

Goldenvoice is putting a two-day music festival right on the sand, the first big beach show since the old Twilight concerts. The Killers and Olivia Dean headline, with Jack White, Khruangbin, and Sublime on the bill, and the pier as the backdrop. Worth grabbing tickets before the westside catches on. Lineup and details.

Coming up · Sep 22

The Lucas Museum of Narrative Art opens

A decade in the making, finally landing in September.

Opens Tue Sep 22 · Exposition Park, DTLA

George Lucas and Mellody Hobson's long-delayed museum finally opens its doors on September 22: 300,000 square feet and 35 galleries devoted to storytelling art, from comics and illustration to film. One of the biggest cultural openings the city has seen in years. Mark the date.

Special section · Aug 13–16

A whole weekend at LACMA

The museum’s Third Weekend, four days deep, around the new David Geffen Galleries.

Thu Aug 13–Sun Aug 16 · LACMA, 5905 Wilshire Blvd · free–$10

Thursday: Artists Take on L.A, with rafa esparza, Shizu Saldamando, and Susan Silton on how this city shapes their work, 7pm, $10. Friday: a free art + meditation session at 10am, then Jazz at LACMA with the Joshua White Quartet, 6pm, free. Saturday: chess at noon, Latin Sounds with the Luis Conte Band at 5pm, and a free screening of The Toll of the Sea in the evening. Sunday: Sketch in the Sculpture Garden at 11am, a Japanese tea ceremony, and a Humberto Leon talk to close it out. The full lineup.

That's the week. And if you want first dibs on the next Long Table, reply to the email and say the word. — Michael
