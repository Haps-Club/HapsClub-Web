One Friday, Two Nights

Cocktails on the sand at five, eighteen miles on foot at eleven, and seventeen days of jazz in between.

A short extra note, because two things on Friday the 21st are worth the calendar now. One is ours, the Sunset Happy Hour. The other asks more of you than anything we have run. You can do both, and a few of us will.

Then the rest of August, starting with a film worth your evening. Tips land first in the Haps WhatsApp group.

The lead pick · Friday, August 21

Haps Club Sunset Happy Hour

Our summer series lands at a beachfront hotel.

Fri Aug 21 · 5–8 PM · a luxury hotel in Santa Monica · from $12

Join us for our Summer Happy Hour Series at a luxury beachfront hotel. Sip, mingle, and unwind with amazing ocean views as the sun sets over the coast. Happy hour runs from 5:00 to 8:00, with a live DJ setting the vibe all evening long. Dress is your summer best. Grab a ticket.

Haps pick · Friday, August 21

The Midnight Walk with The Midnight Mission

Eighteen miles through a city you have never seen this quiet.

Fri Aug 21 · meet 11 PM · Expo/Sepulveda Station, 11295 Exposition Blvd · free to walk, donations welcome

An overnight walk in solidarity with our unhoused neighbors, ending back at the Expo platform as the sun comes up. My good friend Ari Wintraub has been doing this one for years, which is how it got on our radar. Dogs welcome, layers advised, broken-in shoes required. Every dollar funds meals, shelter, and recovery programs. Sign up or support a walker.

Happening now · In theaters

Cookie Queens

Girl Scout cookie season, taken as seriously as it deserves.

Opens in theaters next week · Roadside Attractions · ticketed

A Haps Club member put it in the group recommending everyone go see it. A coming-of-age story about the joys and quiet pressures of Girl Scout cookie season, and an utterly delightful watch. Find a showtime.

Happening now · Aug 7–23

The LA Jazz Festival, all seventeen days

Leimert Park to Dockweiler Beach, block parties to late nights.

Fri Aug 7 – Sun Aug 23 · venues across Southern California · free and ticketed

Fifty-plus events, 150-plus artists, and a good chunk of it free: concerts, block parties, and late-night sets from Leimert Park to the beach. Headliners include John Legend, Janelle Monáe, Raphael Saadiq, Charlie Wilson, and Parliament Funkadelic with George Clinton, plus Ezra Collective and Nubya Garcia. Browse the run and pick your nights early.

Happening now · Wed–Sun through Sep 6

Free Shakespeare at the Old Zoo

The Comedy of Errors, outdoors, five nights a week, for nothing.

Wed–Sun through Sep 6 · 7 PM · The Dell at the Old Zoo, Griffith Park · free

Independent Shakespeare Co. opened its Griffith Park run with the silliest thing in the canon, original score and plenty of slapstick. Bring a blanket, a picnic, and a layer, and arrive early for decent grass. The full schedule.

Coming up · Friday, August 14

Modern by Moonlight on Bunker Hill

Downtown’s skyline walking tour, moved to twilight.

Fri Aug 14 · 7 PM · meets at Maguire Gardens, DTLA · $25, $18 members · about 2 hours, 1 mile

The LA Conservancy walks its Modern Skyline tour after dark, through Bunker Hill’s towers and plazas as the lights come on. The Aug 7 date is already sold out, so the 14th is the one to book. Steps and hills, wear something sensible. Reserve a spot.

Coming up · Aug 14–28

dineL.A. Restaurant Week

Two weeks to eat somewhere you have been putting off.

Fri Aug 14 – Fri Aug 28 · 400+ restaurants · prix fixe from $15

Fixed-price lunch and dinner menus at 400-plus restaurants, tiered from $15 past $65 — the cheapest way into a room you would otherwise save for an anniversary. Book the good ones the day menus drop. Menus and reservations.

AWOL · Always West of Lincoln · Santa Monica

Wellness & Waves on the pier

A group run, then yoga at the end of the pier, every Saturday.

Saturdays through Oct 17 · 8–10 AM · Santa Monica Pier · free, register ahead

Group run at eight, one to three miles, meeting under the Yacht Harbor sign, then yoga or mat pilates at nine on the west end with water on three sides. Free, all levels. Bring a mat and be done before the pier fills up. How it works.

AWOL · Venice

Venice Art Crawl in the canals

Art in front rooms and gardens along the water.

Thu Aug 20 · 6–10 PM · the historic Venice canals and nearby venues · free to walk

The canal-district edition is the one to catch: artists showing in homes and gardens along the footbridges, at the hour the light on the water does the work. Grab the map the day of, since the roster grows until the night before. Details.

AWOL · Playa del Rey

Jazz on the Beach at Dockweiler

The festival’s closing weekend, on the sand, planes overhead.

Sat Aug 22 – Sun Aug 23 · Dockweiler Beach · ticketed, single-day and both-day

The festival closes on the widest stretch of beach on the Westside, ten to nine both days. A proper beach festival with a jazz spine, so plan a full day and a hat. Single-day tickets if you only want one. Lineup and tickets.

That is the 21st, and the three weeks around it. If you are walking the eighteen miles, say so in the WhatsApp group so nobody starts alone. — Michael
