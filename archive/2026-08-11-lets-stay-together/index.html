● Tuesday, August 11, 2026 · Let’s stay together

Let’s Stay Together

Al Green under the stars, two weeks of prix fixe, and a slumber party at the cemetery.

This week the city leans hard into Friday and Saturday, so plan accordingly. And a reminder that our own Sunset Happy Hour lands Friday the 21st, oceanfront in Santa Monica.

The LA Jazz Festival is still rolling citywide through the 23rd, much of it free. Tips land first in the Haps WhatsApp group.

The lead pick · Starts Friday

dineL.A. Restaurant Week

Two weeks to eat somewhere you have been putting off.

Fri Aug 14 – Fri Aug 28 · 400+ restaurants · prix fixe from $15

Fixed-price lunch and dinner menus at 400-plus restaurants, tiered from $15 past $65 — the cheapest way into rooms like Spago, Mélisse, and Girl & the Goat. Menus are live now, and the good tables go the first weekend. Browse and book.

Friday, August 14

Al Green at the Hollywood Bowl

The Reverend, one night, under the open sky.

Fri Aug 14 · 8 PM · Hollywood Bowl · ticketed

Eleven Grammys, a Rock & Roll Hall of Fame plaque, and the catalog that soundtracked half the slow dances in history. The Womack Sisters open. Pack a picnic and take the shuttle. Tickets.

Saturday, August 15

Cinespia’s Slumber Party: X + Pearl

A Mia Goth double feature, in a cemetery, on purpose.

Sat Aug 15 · gates 6:45 PM, film 8:30 PM · Hollywood Forever Cemetery · ticketed

The annual slumber party edition of the cemetery screenings goes gloriously blood-soaked this year, with a dance party, free photo booth, and a beer and wine bar on the lawn. Not one for the faint of heart, which is rather the point. Details and tickets.

Friday, August 14 · Final date

Modern by Moonlight, last call

Downtown’s skyline walking tour, moved to twilight.

Fri Aug 14 · 7 PM · meets at Maguire Gardens, DTLA · $25, $18 members · about 2 hours, 1 mile

The LA Conservancy walks its Modern Skyline tour after dark, through Bunker Hill’s towers and plazas as the lights come on. This Friday is the last of only three dates this season. Steps and hills, wear something sensible. Reserve a spot.

AWOL · Thursday, August 13

The last Sunset Picnic at the Beach House

Bring a basket to the old Marion Davies estate one more time.

Thu Aug 13 · 5–8 PM · Annenberg Community Beach House, 415 PCH · free, RSVP encouraged

The final one of the year: bring your own picnic, settle in for live music from Caro Pierotto, games, a photobooth, and s’mores while they last. Come solo and join a communal table, or bring the whole crew. RSVP free.

AWOL · Friday, August 14

Premium Pours & Grilled Cheese Night

Rare bottles and golden sandwiches on Montana Avenue.

Fri Aug 14 · 7–9:30 PM · Andrew’s Cheese Shop, 728 Montana Ave · ticketed, 21+

Andrew walks you through four courses of grilled cheese, each paired with allocated beers, sparkling wines, and a few beer-wine hybrids you will not find on shelves, capped with a Basque cheesecake pairing. These sell out fast. Grab seats.

AWOL · Saturdays through Oct 17

Wellness & Waves on the pier

A group run, then yoga at the end of the pier, every Saturday.

Saturdays through Oct 17 · 8–10 AM · Santa Monica Pier · free, register ahead

Group run at eight, one to three miles from the Yacht Harbor sign, then yoga or mat pilates at nine on the west end with water on three sides. Free, all levels, done before the pier fills up. How it works.
