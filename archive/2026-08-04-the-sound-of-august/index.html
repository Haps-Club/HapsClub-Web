The Sound of August

Free gospel-soul in a hillside courtyard, jazz on the LACMA plaza, a block party at the end of Washington Blvd, and a night swim in a 1920s pool.

Hi, we are still hearing about the Long Table around the neighborhood, and the next one is quietly taking shape in the WhatsApp group, where it will land first.

This week the city is generous with free music: the Skirball’s season finale on Thursday, jazz on the LACMA plaza on Friday, and a whole afternoon of it at the Venice Pier on Saturday.

The lead pick · This Thursday

Sunset Concerts finale at the Skirball

Disco soul and sacred steel to close season 29.

Thu Aug 6 · doors 6 PM, sets 7 and 8:15 PM · Skirball Cultural Center, 2701 N Sepulveda Blvd · free, walk-up · parking $20

The Skirball closes its 29th summer with Annie and The Caldwells, an intergenerational family band playing powerful disco soul, and The Campbell Brothers, masters of the sacred steel tradition, plus a DJ set from Sonrisita. Admission is free and first-come, so arrive early and watch the sun drop behind the 405. Details and the full lineup.

This week · Thursday

Pop, soul, and blues at the Culver Steps

Downtown Culver City's Thursday-night habit.

Thu Aug 6 · 7–9 PM · Town Plaza at The Culver Steps, 9300 Culver Blvd · free

Culver City's Summer Sunset series has Soul3Sixty plus Lester Lands and Sonny Green with The Midnight Blues Revue, ringed by all the Culver Steps restaurants. Two more Thursdays left after this one. The full schedule.

This week · Friday

Jazz at LACMA: Theo Saunders Septet

A 35-year-old Friday tradition, still free.

Fri Aug 7 · 6 PM · Smidt Welcome Plaza, LACMA · free, no tickets needed

Pianist Theo Saunders brings a septet to the plaza for the museum’s long-running Friday series. Bring a picnic, stay for golden hour, and wander the new David Geffen Galleries while you are there. Series details.

AWOL · Always West of Lincoln · Venice

The Venice Pier Block Party

The whole pier district throws a party, one Saturday only.

Sat Aug 8 · daylong · Venice Pier, 1 Washington Blvd · free

The Venice Pier Association’s rescheduled block party lands this Saturday: live music, DJs, local artists, a photo booth, and specials from the pier-district regulars. The rundown from Yo Venice. If you drift toward the Marina after, Burton Chace Park has a free summer concert the same evening, with food trucks from 5 PM.

AWOL · Santa Monica

Sunset Swim at the Annenberg Beach House

Marion Davies's 1920s pool, after hours, adults only.

Fri Aug 7 · 6–9 PM · Annenberg Community Beach House, 415 PCH · 18+ · regular pool admission

The Beach House opens its heated oceanfront pool for an evening swim with s’mores and floaties provided, in the pool William Randolph Hearst built for Marion Davies. Passes at the door or online ahead of time. The details.

AWOL · Santa Monica

West Side Oyster Club on Ocean Ave

Lobster rolls and chowder bread bowls above the Pacific.

Open now · 1355 Ocean Ave, Santa Monica · counter service

The family behind Rusty’s on the pier, west side since 1977, opened a fast-casual raw bar up Ocean Ave: oysters four ways, two styles of lobster roll, and their clam chowder in a bread bowl. Caviar to go, if that is your Saturday. The menu.

Coming up · Friday, August 21

The Midnight Walk with The Midnight Mission

Eighteen miles through a city you have never seen this quiet.

Fri Aug 21 · meet 11 PM · Expo/Sepulveda Station, 11295 Exposition Blvd · free to walk, donations welcome

An overnight walk in solidarity with our unhoused neighbors, ending back where it starts as the sun comes up. Dogs are welcome, layers are advised, and every dollar raised funds meals, shelter, and recovery programs. Sign up or support the walk.

Coming up · Saturday, August 15

Cinespia’s Slumber Party: X and Pearl

A double feature on the lawn, before it sells out too.

Sat Aug 15 · gates 6:45 PM · film 8:30 PM · Hollywood Forever Cemetery · ticketed

This Saturday’s screening of The Mummy is already sold out, which tells you how these go. The following weekend’s Mia Goth double feature, with a dance party, photo moments, and giveaways, still has tickets. Grab them while they last.

That’s the week. If you want first word on the next Long Table, the WhatsApp group hears it before anyone else. — Michael
