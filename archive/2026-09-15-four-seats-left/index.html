● Tuesday, September 15, 2026

Four Seats Left

Four seats are left at Saturday's long table, Sophocles runs in Nahuatl at the Getty Villa, and Saturday night makes you choose between a brass band and a rooftop Truman Show.

Our long-table dinner on Saturday is down to its last handful of seats, so this one leads with it. After that: trivia tonight in Mid City, Sophocles in Nahuatl at the Villa, and a Saturday night that makes you choose.

Haps pick · South LA

Masala Meets Mexico

A long table, two street-food traditions, one candlelit night.

Sat Sep 19 · 6–9pm · private residence, South LA · $79

The Haps Club x Long Table dinner series is back, and this one is playful: a multi-course Indian and Mexican vegetarian street-food menu, eaten with your hands. Cardamom horchata, hibiscus-tamarind pani puris, grilled paneer tostadas. Candles, music, communal seating, and a room small enough that you leave knowing everyone in it. Hosted with Anjali Banthia.

Alcohol-free evening, easy residential street parking nearby. Take one of the last seats.

Tonight · Mid City

KCRW Trivia at Re/creation Cafe

Paper, pen, musical clues, no table holding.

Tue Sep 15 · 6–8pm · Re/creation Cafe, 4500 W Washington Blvd · RSVP

A standing Tuesday at the Mid City cafe, presented by KCRW and hosted by Lucky Guess Trivia. Come solo and they will find you a team. Tables are first come first served, and it repeats every Tuesday. RSVP.

Thursday through Saturday · Pacific Palisades

Antigone: In the Language of Kings

Sophocles relocated to Tenochtitlan, outdoors, after dark.

Thu–Sat through Oct 3 · 8pm · Getty Villa Outdoor Theater · $30 previews, $45–$60 general

The Villa's outdoor classical theater turns twenty this year, and marks it with Evelina Fernández's adaptation, directed by José Luis Valenzuela and co-produced with Latino Theater Company. Performed in English, Spanish and Nahuatl, with supertitles, woven through with pre-Hispanic music and poetry.

Ninety minutes, no intermission. Check-in opens at 6pm, seating at 7:15, general admission. Bring a layer. Tickets and dining.

Saturday night · Mid City

The Big Ass Brass Band

New Orleans horns, two hours, Washington Boulevard.

Sat Sep 19 · 7:30–9:30pm · Re/creation Cafe, 4500 W Washington Blvd

Quinn Carson brings the full horn section back for the cafe's Saturday night live music slot. Small room, big band, and that mismatch is the entire appeal. RSVP.

Saturday night · Downtown

The Truman Show on a Roof

A film about a fake sky, under a real one.

Sat Sep 19 · doors 6:25pm, film at 7:25pm · Rooftop Cinema Club, LEVEL DTLA · 16+

Peter Weir's 1998 film holds up uncomfortably well in 2026, and a deck chair above Downtown with wireless headphones is the right way to find that out. Book a seat.

AWOL · Santa Monica

A Proper Night Out

Venice Beach Wine Club takes over a hotel bar for the night.

Wed Sep 16 · 7pm · Palma at Santa Monica Proper Hotel · tickets from about $6

The Venice Beach Wine Club crowd moves up to Santa Monica for a wine night at Palma, with a curated list, bites and DJ Loren Albritton on the room. Get a ticket.

AWOL · Venice

Long Run Saturday

Six miles or ten, off Dell Avenue, before the beach fills up.

Sat Sep 19 · 8:30am · parking lot at 2150 Dell Ave, Venice · free, no sign-up

Venice Run Club's Saturday long run splits into a six-mile group and a ten-plus group, so the pace question sorts itself out at the start line. Mobbin Wednesday does 4.5 miles from the same spot at 6:30pm. Run schedule.

AWOL · Santa Monica

Diving the Great Barrier Reef

The night the reef spawns, told by someone who was in the water.

Thu Sep 17 · 7:30pm · BroadStage, 1310 11th St, Santa Monica · 75 minutes

BroadStage opens its 2026/27 season with a Nat Geo Live night from marine biologist Erika Woolsey, who dives the reef on the nights millions of coral polyps spawn at once. The other half of the talk is harder: more than half the coral there has died. Tickets.
