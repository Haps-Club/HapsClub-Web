The hardest part is starting

Every so often, I'm going to write a little about myself.

I've always been creative, but more through problem-solving than painting. My career has lived somewhere between creative and analytical: licensed architect, nearly 15 years running a digital marketing business, real estate investor, and now Realtor.

And recently, I started BizColab — Empowering Entrepreneurs Through Collaboration. The idea is simple: people benefit from collaborating outside their immediate circles, and everyone has something valuable to offer.

Even after 20+ years of entrepreneurship, getting the first six people to join was a slog. My invitation sat in my inbox for six weeks. It reminded me of something I say all the time: the hardest part is starting — and sticking with it. No amount of social media or marketing replaces picking up the phone and telling someone, “I think you'd be great for this.”

So I did. And people said yes.

That's the point of this unsolicited life coaching: whenever you start something new, you're a beginner again. There's a learning curve, some awkwardness, and plenty of uncertainty. But the upside is that every win feels meaningful again. Going from one customer to 100 can be far more gratifying than growing an established business another 10%.
