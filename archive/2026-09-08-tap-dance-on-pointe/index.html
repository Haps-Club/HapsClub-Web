● Tuesday, September 8, 2026

Tap Dance on Pointe

The LA Phil and LA Ballet share a stage for the first time tonight, plus a Thursday triple-header and a new bodega in Venice.

The LA Phil and LA Ballet share a stage for the first time tonight, Thursday turns into a genuine problem of choice, and there's a new bodega in Venice worth a detour.

Haps pick · Hollywood

LA Ballet Makes Its Bowl Debut

The Phil and the ballet, sharing a stage for the first time ever.

Tue Sep 8 · gates 6pm, music at 8pm · Hollywood Bowl

Los Angeles Ballet teams up with the LA Phil for the first time in the company's history, with Grammy-winning conductor Carlos Miguel Prieto leading a program of American and Mexican works — Copland's Rodeo, Moncayo's Huapango, and a new Melissa Barak ballet set to Gabriela Ortiz's Kauyumari.

The one to watch for is Balanchine's Who Cares?, a jazzy set of Gershwin songs the company itself describes as tap dance on pointe. See the program.

Thursday, three ways · Hollywood

Ravel, Debussy & Saint-Saëns

Jean-Yves Thibaudet plays the Bowl's favorite piano concerto.

Thu Sep 10 · 8pm · Hollywood Bowl

Conductor Philippe Jordan and Bowl favorite Jean-Yves Thibaudet take on Ravel's Piano Concerto in G, then La valse and Debussy's Clair de lune after intermission. Hollywood Bowl.

Thursday, three ways · Topanga

Beer, A New Musical

A staged reading, under the oaks, from Inara George.

Thu Sep 10 · 7:30pm · Will Geer's Theatricum Botanicum, 1419 N Topanga Canyon Blvd · $35

Two knights, a priest and a farmer plot to steal a coveted recipe in this musical-in-development by Inara George (The Bird and the Bee) and Jennifer Chang, performed by current and recent UCLA theater grads. Get tickets.

Thursday, three ways · Downtown

Downtown LA Art Walk

Galleries and pop-ups, second Thursday of the month.

Thu Sep 10 · 6–10pm · Spring & Main Streets, Historic Core · free

The Historic Core's galleries, studios and pop-ups stay open late, with a free map at the Art Walk Lounge on Spring Street. Downtown Art Walk.

AWOL · Venice

Bodega + Palms

The old Mitchell's Market, reborn as a proper neighborhood bodega.

Open now · 1915 Penmar Ave, Venice · Mon–Sat, 8am–8pm

A 100-year-old corner market redone as a coffee bar and grocery, with local vendors on the shelves, sandwiches, and a small wine selection. Bodega + Palms.

AWOL · Santa Monica

Watch AA Volleyball on the Sand

Open, AA and A division matches, right on the public courts.

Sun Sep 13 · all day · Ocean Park, Santa Monica

The CBVA runs Women's Open, Men's AA and Women's A brackets on the public sand at Ocean Park — no ticket needed. CBVA tournament schedule.

AWOL · Santa Monica

One Last Lap at Annenberg

The oceanfront pool's extended season, weekends only.

Sat & Sun through Sep 27 · Annenberg Community Beach House · day passes at the door

The heated pool on the old Marion Davies estate stays open weekends through the end of the month, passes first-come at the guest services window. Annenberg Community Beach House.
