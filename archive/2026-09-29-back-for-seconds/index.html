Back for Seconds

Back to Hermanito Broadway for dinner Thursday, the Lucas Museum is finally open, and the Westside is loaded this weekend with Pier Play and First Friday.

Happy hour at Hermanito left the room wanting more, so we booked it again, this time for a proper sit-down dinner this Thursday, and tickets are still open if you want in. Also on deck: the long-awaited Lucas Museum of Narrative Art is finally open in Exposition Park, Culver City's Arts Festival is worth circling for next month, and the Westside is stacked this weekend with Pier Play and First Friday on Abbot Kinney. And ARTBOX is bringing its first LA pop-up to Melrose starting Saturday.

Haps pick · Santa Monica · This Thursday

Haps Club × Hermanito Broadway Dinner Series

We loved the happy hour so much we booked the whole room for dinner.

Thu Oct 1 · 6–9pm · Hermanito Broadway, Santa Monica · from $89

Hermanito's kitchen hosts us family-style: guacamole, papas flautas, sticky ribs, and a spread of tacos, plus dessert. Ticket includes dinner and one drink. 6–6:45 is meet and mingle, dinner runs 6:45–8:15, then dessert and conversation until 9.

Early bird $89, standard $99, $109 after · all sales final, transfers considered by request to michael@haps.club

Shopping · Melrose · Oct 3–11

ARTBOX LA Pop-Up Store

The Korean lifestyle brand's first US pop-up lands on Melrose.

Sat Oct 3 · 4:30–8pm, then Sun Oct 4–Sun Oct 11 · 11am–7pm · 8250 Melrose Ave · open to the public

A week and a half of ARTBOX's design-forward stationery, toys, and everyday objects, built out as a full retail experience rather than a shelf in a gift shop. Free to walk through, no RSVP needed. Map.

Culture · Exposition Park · $25

Lucas Museum of Narrative Art

George Lucas's decades-in-the-making museum has finally opened its doors in Exposition Park.

Now open · 3900 S. Figueroa St, Exposition Park · timed entry, $25 adults, free for 17 and under

More than 40,000 works of visual storytelling, from illustration and comic art to film concept art and fine art, in a building that's been the subject of speculation since it was announced. Book a timed-entry slot before you go, even members need one. Plan your visit.

Culture · Culver City · Free

Culver City Arts Festival

The eighth year of live art, music, and food trucks along Washington Boulevard.

Sat Oct 10 · 11am–6pm · Washington Blvd, Culver City Arts District · free entry

Live art-making, artisan vendors, two music stages, food trucks, a beer garden, and an International Arts Zone take over Washington Blvd between Helms and La Cienega. Parking is tight, so plan on transit, a bike, or rideshare. Details.

Food · Santa Monica · Soft opening

Matilda's Bakery

Bread, pastries, and coffee take over the old Santa Monica Airport admin building.

Fri Oct 2 – Sun Oct 4 · 7am–12pm · 3200 Airport Ave, Santa Monica · soft opening

A four-day process gets every croissant to the counter: mixed and folded overnight, laminated the next day, rolled, rested, then baked. Beans come from Caffe Luxxe a few miles away, butter from Échiré. This is soft opening round two, so expect the hours to be tight. Details.

Food · Santa Monica · Now open

Met Her at a Bar

The couple's sixth spot lands on Main Street, worth it for the pancakes alone.

Open daily · 2651 Main St, Santa Monica · 8am–10pm Sun–Thu, until 11pm Fri–Sat

Mindy and Vinny Kinne's first Santa Monica location folds their whole catalog into one all-day café: Belgian waffles and Thai chicken and waffles in the morning, then Neapolitan pizza, handmade pasta, and a full cocktail list once the sun's down. I was just there, and I do not eat pancakes often, but when I do, this is where I will do it, worth every calorie. Details.

Culture · Santa Monica · From $65

Pier Play

The dance festival on the Santa Monica Pier goes to two days for the first time.

Sat Oct 3 & Sun Oct 4 · 2:30–10:30pm · Santa Monica Pier · 21 and over, from $65

Four sold-out editions later, Pier Play expands to two separate lineups with Meduza, Monolink, Claptone, LP Giobbi, Mind Against, and Kaz James, right on the sand with the Ferris wheel behind the decks. Tickets.

Food · Venice · Free entry

First Friday on Abbot Kinney

Food trucks, boulevard bar hopping, and a proper night out in Venice.

Fri Oct 2 · 5–9:30pm · Abbot Kinney Blvd, Venice · free to attend

A rotating lineup of vetted food trucks lines the boulevard, shops keep late hours, and the whole street turns into a food-truck crawl for the evening. No tickets, just show up hungry. Truck list.

Grab a seat at Hermanito if Thursday works, then spend the weekend bouncing between the pier and Abbot Kinney. The WhatsApp group always hears first if something gets added.
