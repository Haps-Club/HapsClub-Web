Costumes, candlelight, and one last seat

Halloween in WeHo, a jazz costume party, Noche de los Muertos, a new pasta bar on Main Street, and the last call for our supper club.

Halloween lands on a Saturday and Día de los Muertos follows right behind it, so this is a big one. It also means our supper club is this Sunday, and ticket sales close Friday. After that, the next time we see you is the Jazz Age Party on November 21.

Haps pick

Masala Meets Mexico, the Underground Supper Club

The Sunday after Halloween, around one candlelit table.

Sunday, November 1 · 6 to 9pm · private home in South LA · $79 · sales close October 30

A vegetarian walk through Indian and Mexican street food: cardamom horchata, hibiscus-tamarind pani puri, grilled paneer tostadas, and a persimmon and pepita salad, with festive non-alcoholic drinks. Code HAPSCLUBPLUS takes 10% off. Get your ticket.

Haps event

Next up, the Jazz Age Party

Keep the costume energy going, 1926 edition.

Saturday, November 21 · historic home, Los Angeles · $45

A live jazz trio, candlelight, and Jazz Age dress required, in a historic home turning 100. The same code works. Get your ticket.

Halloween weekend

West Hollywood Halloween Carnaval

A mile of Santa Monica Boulevard, and everyone in costume.

Saturday, October 31 · 6 to 11pm · Santa Monica Blvd, Doheny to La Cienega · free, no tickets

LA’s biggest Halloween party, with a DJ stage at San Vicente, food trucks, and more than 100,000 people in costume. No drinking on the street, and only clear bags. The free WeHo Halloween Ride runs along Santa Monica Blvd from 6pm to 3am. Know before you go.

Halloween weekend

Maiya Sykes’ Monster Mash at Vibrato

Halloween, but with a jazz club and a dinner reservation.

Saturday, October 31 · shows at 7 and 9:30pm · Vibrato Grill Jazz, 2930 Beverly Glen Circle

Her third annual costume party at Herb Alpert’s room, with jazz, funk, and disco. Reserve a table.

Halloween weekend

Noche de los Muertos at Grand Park

The altars by candlelight, with a cumbia send-off.

Sunday, November 1 · 3 to 7pm · Gloria Molina Grand Park, 200 N Grand Ave · altars on view daily through November 2

The closing night of Downtown Día, with The Negrete Family, Lxs Cochinxs, and DJ Butchona, plus 17 artist altars. Bring a photocopy of a photo for the community altar. RSVP free.

Bar Fortuna on Main Street

Five Negronis, fresh pasta, and a long marble bar.

2917 Main St, Ocean Park · opened October 21

The new Italian bar from Crudo e Nudo chef Brian Bornemann, a block from his seafood spot. House-made pasta like gemelli with sausage and grapes, a California and Italy wine list, and an espresso window out front. Read more.

AWOL · Always West of Lincoln

The Santa Monica Open, Pickleball Weekend

Play Saturday morning, costume Saturday night.

Saturday, October 31 and Sunday, November 1 · 8am to 3pm · Memorial Park, Olympic Blvd · $30 per player

Saturday is women’s and men’s doubles, Sunday is open and mixed doubles. All levels, and part of the proceeds go to Santa Monica schools. Register.

AWOL · Always West of Lincoln

Día de los Muertos at SPARC

A sunset ritual on Venice Boulevard, in its 24th year.

Sunday, November 1 · 5:30 to 8:30pm · SPARC, 685 Venice Blvd · free

The evening opens with an invocation in Nahuatl, Spanish, and English, then altars, art, performances, and Oaxacan treats. Bring a photo for the community altar, and take it home after. Event details.
