Supper Club, and a Month of Halloween

Our Underground Supper Club returns November 1, plus a month of Halloween, CicLAvia on Sunday, and a free late night at the Hammer.

Thank you to everyone who joined us at our Hermanito dinner on Thursday, which followed our happy hour there. Good food, good people, and a table full of strangers who stopped being strangers by dessert. If you missed our last Masala Meets Mexico dinner, here is a look back. Next up is our Underground Supper Club on November 1, with tickets 10% off this week. This month we're leaning all the way into Halloween: glowing pumpkin trails, a month of horror, and a CicLAvia ride on Sunday. Tickets for our Jazz Age Party on November 21 are live too.

Haps pick · South LA · 10% off this week

Haps Underground Supper Club: Masala Meets Mexico

A cozy vegetarian dinner where Indian and Mexican street food meet at one candlelit table.

Sun Nov 1 · 6–9pm · a private home in South LA · $79 · sales end Oct 30

A playful multi-course journey with a fall twist: cardamom horchata, hibiscus-tamarind pani puri, grilled paneer tostadas, a persimmon and roasted pepita salad, and more. It is hands-on, communal, and meant for meeting new people. Fully non-alcoholic with festive drinks, so no BYOB. Easy street parking nearby, and we share the exact address with confirmed guests.

Twenty-plus museums, galleries, and stages open their doors for free after dark.

Fri Oct 9 · 6–10pm · venues across Pasadena · free entry, free shuttles
