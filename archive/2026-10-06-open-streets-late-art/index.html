● Tuesday, October 6, 2026

Open Streets, Late Art

CicLAvia opens downtown streets Sunday, ArtNight lights up Pasadena Friday, and the Westside gets a free late night at the Hammer.

Thank you to everyone who joined us at Hermanito on Thursday. Good food, good people, and a table full of strangers who stopped being strangers by dessert. We're already planning the next one, and the Haps WhatsApp group hears first.

Haps pick

CicLAvia: Heart of LA

Six and a half miles of car-free streets, and the whole city out on them.

Sun Oct 11 · 9am–4pm · Chinatown, Little Tokyo, Arts District, Boyle Heights, Echo Park · free

Walk, bike, skate, or scooter a route that links Chinatown, Little Tokyo, the Arts District, Boyle Heights, and Echo Park. Hop on anywhere along the course. See the route.

Culture

ArtNight Pasadena

Twenty-plus museums, galleries, and stages open their doors for free after dark.

Fri Oct 9 · 6–10pm · venues across Pasadena · free entry, free shuttles

Live dance and music, hands-on workshops, ghost stories, and a pop-up piano bar at the Pasadena Playhouse. Venue list.

Film

Screamfest Horror Film Festival

Ten days of independent horror at the Chinese Theatres, just in time for October.

Tue Oct 6 – Thu Oct 15 · TCL Chinese 6 Theatres, Hollywood · single-day passes and full badges

The 26th edition opens with the world premiere of The Night Driver and an after-party. Schedule and passes.

AWOL · Always West of Lincoln

Hammer Museum Fall Exhibition Celebration

Late-night galleries and a DJ in the courtyard, on the house.

Sat Oct 10 · 8pm · Hammer Museum, 10899 Wilshire Blvd · free, first come first served

The Hammer opens its fall season with retrospectives of Huguette Caland and Mavis Pusey, with DJ Shay Elys in the courtyard. Hammer events.

AWOL · Always West of Lincoln

Bower Surf Club

An Aussie beach house for breakfast, lunch, and a very good rigatoni.

Open daily · 2501 Colorado Ave, Santa Monica · 7:30am–6pm Mon–Fri, 9am–6pm Sat–Sun

Aussie brekkie in the morning, rigatoni bolognese and ginger miso salmon later, with big shared tables and three hours of free validated parking. Menu and Resy.

AWOL · Always West of Lincoln

Bacchus Uncorked: Beer in Ancient Mesopotamia and Egypt

Five thousand years of brewing, with a pour of the modern descendants.

Sat Oct 10 · 5:15–8:30pm · Getty Villa, 17985 Pacific Coast Hwy · 21 and over · $110

An archaeologist and a beer historian trace how the first brewers worked, then the evening moves to the Villa's Cafe Terrace. Tickets.

AWOL · Always West of Lincoln

The Santa Monica Open: Pickleball

Start the doubles partner search now, this one is three weeks out.

Sat Oct 31 & Sun Nov 1 · Memorial Park, Santa Monica · $30 per player, $60 per team

A community tournament with women's and men's doubles on Saturday and open and mixed doubles on Sunday, welcome to all ages and levels. Register.
