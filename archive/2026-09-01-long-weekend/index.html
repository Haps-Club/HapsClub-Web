The Long Weekend

Labor Day gives us one last three-day run at summer — John Williams at the Bowl, movies at Hollywood Forever, and a new raw bar by the pier.

Thanks to everyone who came out to the sunset happy hour. The next one is in the works for mid-September, and details always land in the Haps WhatsApp first. Meanwhile, Labor Day gives us one last three-day run at summer — here is how to spend it.

Haps pick

Maestro of the Movies at the Bowl

Star Wars under actual stars.

Fri Sep 4 & Sat Sep 5 at 8pm, Sun Sep 6 at 7:30pm · Hollywood Bowl

The LA Phil closes the holiday weekend with a three-night tribute to John Williams, conducted by David Newman — Star Wars, Indiana Jones, Jurassic Park and more. Bring a picnic, take the shuttle, stay for the lightsabers on the encore. Hollywood Bowl.

The long weekend

Cinespia's summer finale weekend

Two nights of movies on the mausoleum lawn.

Sat Sep 5 & Sun Sep 6 · Hollywood Forever Cemetery

Josie and the Pussycats on Saturday, The Sandlot on Sunday — DJ sets, photo booth, and blankets and picnics welcome. These are the last lawn screenings before the season winds down. Cinespia.

The long weekend

Latin Sounds closes its season

Afro-Cuban rhythms on the LACMA lawn, no ticket required.

Sat Sep 5 · 5pm · Hancock Park at LACMA · free

Ricardo Lemvo & Makina Loco close out LACMA's free Saturday concert series with a blend of Afro-Cuban and pan-African styles the LA Times called seamless and infectious. Lawn chairs and picnics encouraged. LACMA music.

The long weekend

Dodgers homestand, bobbleheads included

Baseball is the correct way to end a summer.

Sat Sep 5 & Sun Sep 6 · Dodger Stadium · vs Washington Nationals

Saturday's 6:10pm game comes with a Yamamoto bobblehead, Sunday brings “The Catch” bobblehead with Andy Pages and Kiké Hernández. Bobblehead lines form early — plan accordingly. Dodgers promotions.

The long weekend

CatVideoFest at Vidiots

Seventy minutes of cats, for charity.

Fri Sep 4 10:15pm · Sat Sep 5 12:30pm · Sun Sep 6 4:45pm · 4884 Eagle Rock Blvd · $12

The annual compilation of the internet's best cat videos returns, with ticket proceeds going to local cat shelters. A full theater laughing at cats is a better time than it has any right to be. Vidiots.

AWOL — Always West of Lincoln

First Fridays on Abbot Kinney

The boulevard, open late and fully alive.

Fri Sep 4 · 6–10pm · Abbot Kinney Blvd · free

Shops open late, food trucks line the block — Kogi and Cousins Maine Lobster among the regulars — and the whole street turns into a walkable night out. Kick off the long weekend here. First Fridays.

AWOL — Always West of Lincoln

Westside Oyster Club

A new raw bar steps from the pier.

Open now · the old Blue Plate Oysterette space, Ocean Ave

From the team behind The Albright on the pier: oysters raw, grilled or fried, clam chowder bread bowls, and lobster rolls with housemade chips. Post-beach seafood done properly. The Infatuation's new openings.

AWOL — Always West of Lincoln

One last beach day, played properly

Volleyball, paddle tennis, and the Strand before the crowds thin.

All weekend · first come, first served · go early on Monday

The public volleyball courts at Ocean Park and the paddle tennis courts at Venice Beach are free and first come, first served — or rent a bike and ride the Strand from Venice to the pier. Labor Day is the unofficial last day of beach season. Use it.
