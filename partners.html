Who you reach

An audience that leaves the house

Not a follower count. A few thousand Angelenos who read a weekly list of what's on, then actually go — and who bring people with them.

Ways to work together

Start anywhere

Every one starts with a conversation

Newsletter

Be in the week

  • Your place, event or offer featured in the Tuesday email
  • Written in Haps voice, approved by you
  • A tracked link, and the open and click numbers after

Good for a launch, an opening week, or a seasonal push.

Most requested

Host a Haps night

  • You provide the room — we bring the people
  • Anywhere from 60 to 250 guests, promoted across email and social
  • A photo set you keep, and the turnout numbers after

Good for hotels, bars and restaurants with a night to fill.

Dinner series

Take the table

  • Ten to forty seats, a chef's menu, one long table
  • Your product on the table or in the glass
  • A room that talks to you, not past you

Good for spirits, wine and food brands.

Season

Come along for the year

  • A standing presence across the newsletter and the events
  • Your name on the nights we host
  • A real review of what worked, every quarter

Good for brands building an LA presence, not buying a one-off.
