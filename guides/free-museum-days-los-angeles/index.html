Every free museum day in LA, checked

Last checked 7 September 2026 against each museum's own site.

Most free-museum lists for Los Angeles are wrong, and they are wrong in the same three ways: they include a museum that closed in 2020, they list free days for a museum that is shut for renovation until 2027, and they get the Autry's free day wrong.

This page is re-checked at the start of every month against each museum's own website, never against another list.

The always-free list is shorter than you have been told — eight museums, not eleven — and two of the eight require a timed reservation people do not expect.

The short answer

Eight museums in LA are free every day they are open.

The Broad, the Getty Center, the Getty Villa, the Hammer, MOCA Grand Avenue, the California Science Center, Griffith Observatory and the Fowler at UCLA.

That is the whole list.

Free admission does not mean a free visit: the Getty charges $25 to park and the Hammer charges $9, which is more than some museums charge to get in.

The Broad
free general admission · timed ticket recommended, walk-ups available daily · some exhibitions carry a separate charge
Getty Center
free · a timed-entry reservation is required, not optional · parking $25, $15 after 3pm, $10 after 6pm, free Saturdays after 6pm
Getty Villa
free · timed-entry reservation required · closed Tuesdays · parking $25, $15 after 3pm
Hammer Museum
free · no advance reservation needed · parking $9 for the first three hours with validation, $9 flat after 5pm weekdays and all day weekends
MOCA Grand Avenue
free general admission · reservations recommended, not required
California Science Center
permanent galleries free, no reservation · the IMAX theatre and special exhibitions are ticketed separately
Griffith Observatory
free admission · the Samuel Oschin Planetarium is ticketed, $12 adult, and tickets are sold only at the Observatory for that day's shows
Fowler Museum at UCLA
always free · closed Monday and Tuesday · Wed 12–8pm, Thu–Sun 12–5pm · parking $5/hr, $17 daily max
Free on a set day

Seven more, if you time it.

The two most commonly misreported are here.

The Autry's free day is the first Wednesday, not the second Tuesday — that is the single most-repeated error in LA free-museum listings.

And Craft Contemporary's Sunday is pay-what-you-wish rather than free, which matters if you turn up with nothing.

LACMA — second Tuesday of every month
free to all visitors · also free to LA County residents on weekdays after 3pm with valid ID · closed Wednesdays
Natural History Museum — weekdays 3–5pm
free to LA County residents, claimed onsite only, not online · closed the first Tuesday of every month
The Autry — first Wednesday of every month
free access to all exhibitions, 10am–4pm · advance registration encouraged, some dates sell out · free parking
The Huntington — first Thursday of every month
free with advance reservation · tickets release the last Thursday of the month at 9am for the following week and sell out · limit five per household per year
Skirball Cultural Center — every Thursday
free to all · free on-site parking · same-day reservations cannot be made online
Norton Simon — first Friday, 4–7pm
free to all · and free every day for students with valid ID and anyone 18 and under · free parking lot
Craft Contemporary — Sundays
pay what you wish, which is not the same as free · $12 general otherwise · under 12 and EBT holders free
The reservation traps

Free does not mean walk in.

Both Getty sites require a timed-entry reservation. Not recommended — required. Turning up without one is the most common way a free Getty day fails.

The Huntington's free day is the hardest ticket in the city. Tickets release the last Thursday of each month at 9am for the following week, there is a virtual waiting room, they sell out, and you are limited to five per household per year.

The Natural History Museum's free hours are claimed onsite. You cannot book the 3–5pm resident window online.

Skirball's free Thursday cannot be reserved same-day online. Walk-ups are welcome but only a reservation guarantees entry.

Griffith Observatory's planetarium tickets are same-day and in-person only. No online, no phone, no advance purchase — the building is free, the show is not, and you cannot plan it from home.

What we could not verify

Left off on purpose.

The Petersen Automotive Museum lists three different prices across three of its own pages, so we are not printing one — and it has no free-admission day.

Descanso Gardens' Free Tuesday appears to have ended, but nothing on their site says so outright, so we are not claiming either way.

The county-wide Museums Free-For-All is an annual one-day event, not an evergreen programme; the most recent was 22 February 2026.

If you spot something here that has changed, tell us and it gets fixed in the next monthly pass.
