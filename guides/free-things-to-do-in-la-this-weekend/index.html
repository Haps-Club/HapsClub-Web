Free every weekend

The list that does not change.

These are free all year and they are the reason you never actually need a plan in this city.

Museums first, because the free ones in LA are genuinely world-class and people still assume they cost money.

Griffith Observatory
free admission, any day · the planetarium is ticketed and same-day only
The Broad, the Hammer, MOCA Grand and the Fowler
free every day they are open · the Getty is free too, but book the timed ticket
The Venice Canals
free · a mile and a half of bridges most LA natives have never walked
The Santa Monica stairs
Adelaide Dr & 4th St · free, street parking
Annenberg Community Beach House
free to wander the courtyard, splash pad and beach courts · parking fills by 11am
Venice Beach Recreation Center
Ocean Front Walk · free paddle tennis, pickleball and volleyball on the sand
Smorgasburg LA
ROW DTLA, 777 S Alameda St · Sundays 10am–4pm · free entry, then go hungry
The Strand
free · flat and fast from Santa Monica south, on foot, bike or skates

What free leaves out

The costs nobody lists.

Parking is the real price of a free day in LA.

The Getty is free and charges $25 to park; the Hammer is free and charges $9; the Annenberg fills its lot by eleven on a Saturday.

Beach lots on a summer weekend will cost you more than dinner.

The workaround is the same every time: park once, walk more than you meant to, and go early.
