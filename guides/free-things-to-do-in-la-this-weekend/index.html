Free every weekend

The list that does not change.

These are free all year and they are the reason you never actually need a plan in this city.

Museums first, because the free ones in LA are genuinely world-class and people still assume they cost money.

Griffith ObservatoryFreeFree admission, any day. The planetarium is ticketed and same-day only. The Broad, the Hammer, MOCA Grand and the FowlerFreeFree every day they are open. Free if you time it right

Monthly and seasonal, worth a calendar reminder.

Each of these is free but only lands on certain days.

First Friday and second Thursday between them cover most of a month.

First Fridays on Abbot KinneyFreeMonthlyFirst Friday, 5–9:30pm. Shops open late, food trucks on the block.
Downtown LA Art WalkFreeMonthlySecond Thursday of the month. Galleries and pop-ups across downtown.
Grand Performances at California PlazaFreeRSVP350 S Grand Ave, DTLA. A full season of free outdoor concerts in downtown's prettiest water-court plaza.
The Culver Steps summer concertsFreeSeasonalTown Plaza, 9300 Culver Blvd. Thursdays in summer. Summer Salsa Nights at Ivy StationFreeSeasonalCulver City. Beginner lesson at 6:30, then a live band.
What free actually costs

Parking. The Getty is free and charges $25 to park; the Hammer is free and charges $9; the Annenberg fills its lot by eleven. Park once, walk more than you meant to, go early.

What free leaves out

The costs nobody lists.

Beach lots on a summer weekend will cost you more than dinner.

The workaround is the same every time: park once, walk more than you meant to, and go early.
