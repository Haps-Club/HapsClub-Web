Christmas lights in LA, and which are free

Four worth a ticket, four that cost nothing, and the two that are off this year.

Every December the same question goes around: where are the good lights. The honest answer is that LA does two kinds. There are the ticketed walks through gardens and the zoo, which are well made and sell out on weekends, and there are the streets where neighbours have been stringing lights for decades and nobody charges a thing.

Every date and price here was read on the organiser's own page this season. This is an El Niño winter and two big displays have already called it off for weather, so check the page on the day if it has been raining.

Worth a ticket

Four that justify the drive and the timed entry.

Descanso Gardens is the one to book if you only book one. The route is about a mile under the oaks and it is the most grown-up of the four. Pick a weeknight entry and the price drops toward the bottom of the range.

Zoo Lights is the cheapest ticketed option and parking is free, but it is dark on more nights than people expect, including Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve. The Griffith Park train is a fifteen-minute loop for ten dollars, which is exactly what it should be. Tinker Bell's Neverland in Palos Verdes is new this year and runs on timed tickets with a fifteen-minute grace period.

Free

The streets, the harbor and the snow.

Christmas Tree Lane in Altadena lost none of its cedars in the Eaton fire and the lighting on Saturday, December 5 is the best free evening of the season. Go for the craft fair in the afternoon and stay for the lights at six.

Candy Cane Lane asks you to dim your headlights and drive slowly, and on a weekend you are better off parking a few blocks out and walking. The Marina del Rey boat parade is the Westside's one: decorated boats down the Main Channel, and Burton Chace Park fills early. The Grove's fake snow is free and it is what it is.

Off this year

Two that most lists still send you to.

Holiday Road at King Gillette Ranch in Calabasas is taking 2026 off for projected El Niño weather and says it plans to return in 2027. The Naples Island boat parade in Long Beach was cancelled for high tides, and so was its house-decorating contest. Holiday Road's notice is here.

If you only do one thing

Descanso on a weeknight, at the first entry time, then dinner in Montrose or Pasadena on the way home. Book the ticket this week; December weekends go first.

Quick answers

Common questions

What are the best Christmas lights in Los Angeles?

For a ticketed walk, Enchanted: Forest of Light at Descanso Gardens, about a mile under the oaks, Nov 22, 2026 to Jan 9, 2027. For free, Christmas Tree Lane in Altadena and Candy Cane Lane in Woodland Hills, plus the Marina del Rey Holiday Boat Parade on Saturday, Dec 12, 2026.

What are the best free Christmas lights in LA?

Christmas Tree Lane in Altadena (lighting ceremony Sat Dec 5, 2026 at 6pm), Candy Cane Lane in Woodland Hills (lights 6-10pm from the first Saturday in December), the Marina del Rey Holiday Boat Parade (Sat Dec 12, 6-8pm) and The Grove's nightly snowfall at 6pm and 8pm.

How much is LA Zoo Lights?

Tickets start at $19 and parking is free. It runs select nights from Nov 20, 2026 to Jan 10, 2027, and is dark on several nights including Dec 24, 25 and 31.

Is Christmas Tree Lane in Altadena happening after the Eaton fire?

Yes. None of its cedars were lost, and the lighting ceremony is Saturday, Dec 5, 2026 at 6pm at Santa Rosa Ave and Mariposa St, with a free Winterfest craft fair from 3pm.

Is Holiday Road at King Gillette Ranch on this year?

No. Holiday Road is taking 2026 off for projected El Niño weather and plans to return in 2027. The Naples Island boat parade in Long Beach is also cancelled this year.
