How to meet people in LA, without an app

Pick one thing that happens every week and keep going back. Here are fourteen of them.

People say Los Angeles is a hard city to make friends in, and they are half right. It is spread out and everyone drives, so the chance meetings that happen on a New York sidewalk do not happen here. What works instead is repetition: one thing, at the same time every week, until the faces become names.

So this guide is mostly recurring things: weekly runs, six-week classes, monthly cleanups. Going once is a nice evening. Going four times is how you meet people. Every schedule here was read on the group's own page.

Run clubs

The fastest way in, and free.

Run clubs have become the city's default social club, and the good ones welcome walkers and slow runners. Koreatown Run Club meets twice a week and asks nothing of you but showing up. Silver Lake Track Club loops the reservoir on Tuesday nights and ends at the bar it started from. On the Westside, the Los Angeles Running Club runs from Playa Vista on Monday evenings.

Koreatown Run ClubFreeMondays 7pm from Wilshire and Oxford, Thursdays 8pm from Love Hour, 532 N Western Ave · three or five miles · no sign-up, just show up.
Silver Lake Track ClubFreeTuesdays 7pm, a three-mile loop of the reservoir from Red Lion Tavern, 2366 Glendale Blvd · every run free, no sign-up · schedule on their Strava and Instagram.
Los Angeles Running ClubWestsideMondays 6:45pm, run or walk three or five miles from 13150 Bluff Creek Dr, Playa Vista · Wednesday track night 6:30pm in Santa Monica, capped at 40 · check membership on their site.
Classes

Being a beginner together.

Improv is the class people credit most often with finding their friends in LA, because it is built on paying attention to strangers. The Groundlings has a single beginner session for $55 if you want to try before committing. In Pasadena, the Saturday swing dance starts with a half-hour lesson and needs no partner; you will dance with a dozen people before the band starts.

Improv for Beginners, The Groundlings SchoolFrom $55Melrose Ave · one two-and-a-half-hour session for $55, no experience · or Workshop A, six weeks for $365 · new classes posted Tuesdays at 1pm. Saturday swing dance, Pasadena Ballroom Dance Association$2573 N Hill Ave, Pasadena · $25 for a lesson at 7:30pm and a live band 8–11pm · no partner needed · free parking. Intro to Wheel Throwing, Good Dirt LAWestside4505 S Centinela Ave, Del Rey · a two-hour class for six people, $85 · you will all be equally bad at it, which helps.
Dinner with strangers

Let the seating do the introductions.

A long table solves the hardest part of meeting people, which is starting the conversation. Haps Club runs its own dinners for exactly this reason, and our supper clubs guide has the rest of the city's. If you would rather be matched, Timeleft seats you with a handful of strangers and tells you the restaurant shortly before.

Haps Club dinnersWestsideOur own long-table dinners, at Hermanito in Santa Monica and elsewhere · seats go to the newsletter first. TimeleftAppMatched dinners for four to six strangers, Wednesdays and Fridays at 7pm, on the Westside, in WeHo and in NoHo · $19.99 a ticket, you pay for your own meal · the one app worth an exception.
Volunteer

Work alongside someone for two hours.

Volunteering is underrated as a way to meet people, because you talk while your hands are busy and nobody has to think of a question. Big Sunday runs short weekday projects in Hollywood. Heal the Bay cleans a different beach every month, and TreePeople plants trees in neighbourhoods that need them.

Big Sunday, HollywoodFreeWeekday-morning projects, 10–11:30am, from 1741 N Cherokee Ave · Holiday Singalong & Toy Drive Sun Dec 6, 3–5pm. Heal the Bay beach cleanupsFreeWestsideMonthly, on a different LA County beach each time · dates posted by the first of the month and spaces fill. TreePeopleFreeTree planting, trail care and restoration across the county · no experience needed · events posted a month ahead, register as one person. Los Angeles Regional Food BankFreeWarehouse shifts at 1734 E 41st St and two other sites · 14 and over · sign up online, no walk-ins.
Leagues and clubs

Somebody else does the organising.

Rec leagues are not just for people who played sport in school. Volo lets you sign up alone and puts you on a team, and kickball is mostly an excuse for the bar afterwards. For quieter people, a monthly book club at Skylight gives you the same faces every month and something to talk about.

Volo SportsJoin aloneKickball, volleyball, pickleball, soccer and more in Santa Monica, Venice, Mar Vista, Culver City, WeHo and beyond · $95–$165 for a six-to-eight-week season · sign up alone as a free agent and they place you on a team. Book clubs at Skylight BooksMonthly1818 N Vermont Ave, Los Feliz · the Rabbit Hole nonfiction club meets the third Thursday at 7:30pm · the fiction club returns Jan 26, 2027.
If you only do one thing

Choose the one closest to where you live or work, put it in your calendar for the next four weeks, and go every time. Proximity beats the perfect group.

Quick answers

Common questions

How do you make friends in Los Angeles as an adult?

Pick one thing that happens every week and keep going back. LA is spread out, so chance meetings are rare; repetition is what turns faces into names. A free weekly run club, a six-week class or a monthly volunteer shift all work.

What are the best run clubs in LA?

Koreatown Run Club (Mondays 7pm and Thursdays 8pm, no sign-up), Silver Lake Track Club (Tuesdays 7pm around the reservoir, free) and, on the Westside, the Los Angeles Running Club's Monday evening run from Playa Vista.

Can I join a sports league in LA by myself?

Yes. Volo Sports lets you sign up as a free agent and places you on a team, with leagues in Santa Monica, Venice, Mar Vista, Culver City, WeHo and more, at $95 to $165 a season.

How do I have dinner with strangers in LA?

Haps Club hosts long-table dinners, with seats going to newsletter readers first. Timeleft matches four to six strangers for dinner on Wednesdays and Fridays at 7pm, at $19.99 a ticket plus your own meal.

Where can I meet people in LA without an app?

Volunteer with Big Sunday, Heal the Bay or TreePeople, take a beginner improv class at the Groundlings, go to the Saturday swing dance in Pasadena (lesson included, no partner needed), or join a book club at Skylight Books.
