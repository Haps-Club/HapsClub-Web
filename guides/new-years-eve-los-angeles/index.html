New Year's Eve in LA, without the cover charge

Two free countdowns, one good concert, a boat if you want one, and a parade in the morning.

Most New Year's Eve advice in Los Angeles is a list of bars charging two hundred dollars for an open bar you will not use. The better nights are the ones where a lot of people stand in the same place at midnight, and in this city two of the best of those are free.

Times and prices were read on each organiser's own page. A lot of venues had not posted their New Year's plans when we checked, so this page gets re-checked through December.

Free

The two countdowns worth going to.

NYELA at Grand Park is the city's own party: a free stage downtown, projections on City Hall at midnight, and a crowd that takes the train in. The lineup for this year was not up yet.

On the Westside, Marina del Rey does a free glow party at Burton Chace Park and launches fireworks off the south jetty at 11:59pm. You can see them from most of the marina, so a walk along the water works as well as a spot in the park.

Ticketed

If you want a seat and a toast.

The LA Phil's Yacht Rock NYE at Disney Hall is the grown-up choice: Christopher Cross, a jazz orchestra, champagne at the door, and you are out in time to make another party. The dinner cruise out of Marina del Rey puts you on the water for the fireworks. The Queen Mary masquerade in Long Beach is the big dress-up one, with its own fireworks over the harbor.

New Year's Day

Falls on a Friday this time.

The Rose Parade starts at 8am on Colorado Boulevard, and the people who see it best camped on the curb the night before. If that sounds like a lot, a First Day Hike with a ranger or a brunch cruise are the gentler ways to start the year. The Rose Bowl is a playoff quarterfinal this time, and its kickoff is set in early December.

Getting home

The part that ruins the night if you skip it.

Grand Park sits on top of four Metro stations, which is the main argument for going. Last year Metro ran free fares and kept several rail lines running all night, but it has not announced this year's plan, so check metro.net in the last week of December. In Marina del Rey, Mindanao Way closes westbound from 5pm, so park in a county lot and walk in.

If you only do one thing

Take the train to Grand Park. It is free, it is the one place in LA where a few thousand strangers count down together, and you do not have to find parking at 1am.

What we could not verify

Left off on purpose.

The Long Beach waterfront party, Pasadena's New Year's Eve events, jazz club and comedy club New Year's shows, the Little Tokyo Oshogatsu festival and the January polar bear swims had no listing for this year on an official page when we checked. Marina del Rey has run an earlier 9pm show in past years; this year's page lists only midnight.

Quick answers

Common questions

What free New Year's Eve events are there in Los Angeles?

NYELA at Gloria Molina Grand Park downtown, 8pm to 1am on Dec 31, 2026, free with no ticket. On the Westside, the Marina del Rey Fireworks and Glow Party at Burton Chace Park, 7pm to midnight, free with an RSVP.

Where can I watch fireworks in LA on New Year's Eve?

Marina del Rey's free fireworks launch from the south jetty at 11:59pm and are visible across most of the marina. In Long Beach, the Queen Mary puts on fifteen minutes of midnight fireworks over the harbor.

When is the Rose Parade 2027?

Friday, Jan 1, 2027, from 8am to about 10am on a 5.5-mile route in Pasadena, mostly along Colorado Boulevard. Watching from the curb is free; overnight street parking opens at noon on Dec 31.

Is LA Metro free on New Year's Eve?

Not announced yet for this year. Last year Metro ran free fares and kept several rail lines running all night, so check metro.net in the last week of December.

Is there a New Year's Eve concert in Los Angeles?

The LA Phil presents A Yacht Rock NYE with Christopher Cross at Walt Disney Concert Hall on Dec 31, 2026 from 7pm. Tickets start at $101 and the first 350 guests get champagne.
