The holidays in LA, done properly

Two outdoor rinks, two Nutcrackers, a room of two thousand people singing Handel, and the free show that moved.

December in Los Angeles is not short of things to do. It is short of the right ones, because the city's holiday calendar is mostly shopping centres with a tree. This is the other list: the rooms where a few hundred people turn up to do the same seasonal thing together, which is most of the point of the season.

Dates and prices were read on each organiser's own page. Christmas lights have their own guide, and so does New Year's Eve.

Ice skating

Two outdoor rinks this year.

Downtown's rink in Pershing Square is the classic: skyline on every side, an hour on the ice, and on weeknights it opens at three. On the Westside, the Santa Monica rink is back as Frost on 5th, a block from the Wednesday farmers market. Burbank and Glendale have not brought theirs back.

On stage

The Nutcracker twice, and Dickens in Pasadena.

Los Angeles Ballet does the full production with a live orchestra, first at Royce Hall and then a longer run at the Dolby. American Contemporary Ballet does the opposite: a small room downtown where you sit close enough to hear the shoes. A Noise Within has made its Christmas Carol a Pasadena habit, and one Thursday is pay what you choose.

Sing along

The best kind of audience participation.

The Messiah Sing-Along is the one people talk about afterwards: the whole of Disney Hall sings the choruses, scores in laps, and nobody minds if you come in late on the Hallelujah. The LA Phil's afternoon sing-along is the lighter version. The Gay Men's Chorus does Dolly Parton this year at the Saban, two shows only.

Free

Including the one that changed its date.

The L.A. County Holiday Celebration has been a Christmas Eve fixture for decades. This year it is on Sunday, December 20, for the first time, with two shows. It is free, and the reservations that guarantee a seat are released at 10am on December 8, 9 and 10. It still airs on PBS SoCal on Christmas Eve at 3pm.

The Unique Markets holiday market is calling this one its last, after nineteen years. It is the place to buy a present from the person who made it.

Christmas week

What is still on, Dec 21 to 27.

The Pershing Square rink runs noon to 10pm every day from December 21 through January 3. On Thursday, December 24, LA Ballet has a 2pm matinee at the Dolby and A Noise Within closes its Christmas Carol at 2pm. LA Ballet picks up again on Saturday the 26th.

On Christmas Day itself, check before you go: most venues on this page have not published Christmas Day hours.

If you only do one thing

Reserve the free Music Center show at 10am on December 8, then skate Pershing Square afterwards. It is a five-minute walk and the whole afternoon costs the price of the rink.

What we could not verify

Left off on purpose.

Las Posadas on Olvera Street, the Kwanzaa parade in Leimert Park and the public menorah lightings usually happen, but none had a listing for this season on an official page when we checked. The Naples boat parade in Long Beach is cancelled. Debbie Allen's Hot Chocolate Nutcracker lists December dates with no year, and Christmas Carol Goes Wrong at the Ahmanson sat behind a queue page, so both are out until we can read them.

Quick answers

Common questions

Where can I ice skate outdoors in Los Angeles?

Downtown at the Holiday Ice Rink in Pershing Square, daily Nov 12, 2026 to Jan 3, 2027, $23.32 with skates. On the Westside at Frost on 5th in Santa Monica, the old Ice at Santa Monica, $22 with skates.

Is the LA County Holiday Celebration still on Christmas Eve?

Not in person. This year it is on Sunday, Dec 20, 2026 at the Dorothy Chandler Pavilion, with free shows at 11am and 3pm. Reservations open at 10am on Dec 8, 9 and 10. It still airs on PBS SoCal on Christmas Eve at 3pm.

Where can I see The Nutcracker in LA?

Los Angeles Ballet plays Royce Hall Dec 11-13 and the Dolby Theatre Dec 18-27 with a live orchestra, including a 2pm Christmas Eve matinee. American Contemporary Ballet's Nutcracker Suite runs Nov 28 to Dec 24 downtown.

When is the Messiah Sing-Along at Disney Hall?

The LA Master Chorale's Great Messiah Sing-Along is Sunday, Dec 20, 2026 at 7pm at Walt Disney Concert Hall. The LA Phil's lighter Holiday Sing-Along is Saturday, Dec 19 at 11:30am and 2:30pm.

What is open in LA on Christmas Eve?

The Pershing Square rink is open noon to 10pm from Dec 21 through Jan 3. LA Ballet has a 2pm Nutcracker matinee at the Dolby on Dec 24, and A Noise Within's A Christmas Carol closes that day at 2pm.
