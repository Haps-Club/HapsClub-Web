Ideas to get more from it

Open to your ideas too

Pay the fee in gift cards

Gift cards cost you less than cash. We give them to guests as raffle prizes, so winners come back, often with friends.

A come-back offer

A card at every seat: dessert or a drink on the house within 30 days. Easy to track how many come back.

Use your slow nights

Monday to Wednesday works best. You fill seats that would sit empty.

Make it a series

One dinner a month or a quarter. Regulars return, and each night gets easier to sell.

Meet the chef

Five minutes from the chef on the menu gives guests a story to share and great content.

Upgrade at the bar

Offer a pairing or second drink for guests to buy. Extra revenue, 90% yours.
